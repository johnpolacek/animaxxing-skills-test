Motion that knows when to stop

Animaxxing is a small studio that builds interfaces for people who read them. We are interested in the quiet part of motion design: the moment a page arrives, the moment it leaves, and the half second in between where most of the damage gets done.

Every screen here runs the same lifecycle. Content is laid out first and animated second, so nothing jumps, nothing flashes, and nothing is hidden from a reader whose script never loaded.
