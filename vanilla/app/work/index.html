Selected work

A reading app for a national archive, where every transition had to survive a slow connection and a screen reader. An events site with a booking flow that never once moved a button out from under a thumb.

A documentation platform whose search results animate in but stay copyable the whole time. A gallery whose images reserve their space long before they arrive.

The through line is restraint. If the motion cannot explain itself in a sentence, it does not ship.
